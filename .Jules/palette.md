# Palette's Journal

## 2025-05-18 - Icon-Only Buttons Focus & Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in the game HUD and modal screens often lack explicit `aria-label` attributes and focus-visible indicators for keyboard users.
**Action:** Always pair icon-only buttons with `aria-label`, `title`, and `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-accent outline-none` styles.
